Course Enrollment

The rules and deadlines governing how courses are added, dropped, and withdrawn in the College of Arts & Sciences.

Planning which courses to take? See Degree Requirements for the distribution categories, or Choosing a Major / Minor for major planning. This page covers enrollment policy and deadlines.

A&S offers roughly 2,000 courses; another 2,000+ are available across Cornell's other colleges. Not all courses outside A&S count toward the degree — see Courses That Won't Count.

Credit-Counting Rules

  • Courses outside A&S departments may or may not count toward A&S degree credit — check Courses That Won't Count before enrolling
  • Courses with overlapping content cannot both be counted toward your degree
  • S/U-graded courses can fulfill distribution requirements, but not if the course also counts toward a major/minor requirement or is a major prerequisite
  • Distribution category codes carry the -AS suffix (e.g., ALC-AS) — a course without that suffix will not fulfill an A&S distribution requirement

Add / Drop / Withdraw Deadlines

WindowRule
AddFirst 15 calendar days of the semester (some courses have special deadlines)
DropFirst 57 days of the semester, if no academic integrity issue is at stake. Dropping removes the course from the transcript entirely.
Withdraw (after day 57)Requires a petition to the college. If approved, course shows a "W" on the transcript in place of a grade — this is permanent and cannot be petitioned after the fact.
Short coursesDeadlines scale proportionally to course length
Academic integrity hold: a student charged with violating the Code of Academic Integrity in a course may not drop that course without written permission from the instructor(s), unless cleared of the charges.
The effective date of any course change is the day the student submits all required, completed paperwork to the A&S Registrar's Office.
