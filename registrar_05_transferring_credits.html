Guidelines for Current A&S Students

  • Credit accepted from regionally accredited institutions, on recommendation of the relevant academic department
  • Minimum grade of "C" required (some departments require higher); ungraded/pass-fail accepted with written C-equivalent confirmation
  • You cannot receive more transfer credit than the offering institution awarded for the course
  • High school college courses may transfer only if: open to all registered students, held on a college campus with matriculated students, taught by college faculty (including adjunct), and not counted toward your high school diploma or Cornell admission requirements
  • Transfer credits count toward the 120 total credits but not the 100 College of Arts & Sciences credits, and don't factor into Cornell GPA
  • Courses taken while on a leave of absence cannot reduce your residence requirement
  • Study abroad credit while on leave is not allowed
  • The Knight Writing Institute determines whether a transfer course satisfies the FWS requirement
  • Transfer credit cannot be applied to the language or distribution requirements
  • Major credit requires department approval — recommended before enrolling in the course
  • During fall/spring terms, you cannot be simultaneously enrolled at Cornell and an external institution (unless on an official exchange) — such courses are ineligible for transfer credit

Requesting a Credit Transfer

1
Complete the Application for Credit from Other Institutions
One application per course requested.
2
Get departmental approval
Check the relevant department's website for review instructions; obtain their sign-off on the application.
3
Get major advisor approval (if applicable)
Required if requesting the credit count toward your major.
4
Submit to the A&S Registrar's Office
Via email or in person at KG17 Klarman Hall.
5
Send the official transcript
After course completion, have the offering institution send an official transcript via email or mail to KG17 Klarman Hall.

Summer Session Credits

  • Summer sessions (Cornell or elsewhere) do not count toward the residence requirement
  • Students may earn up to 12 credits in one summer
  • Cornell summer session credits in A&S courses can fulfill college requirements, including FWS and language
