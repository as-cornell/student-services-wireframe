Applying to Graduate

Applications are submitted during your penultimate semester (the semester before your final one). Spring graduates apply in the preceding fall.

1
Submit Part I — College Requirements
Complete the online form via data.arts. Includes your DUST report and a term-by-term course/credit chart. You'll answer questions about fulfillment of each college requirement. You'll receive an email confirmation.
2
Meet with your faculty advisor — Part II (Major Requirements)
Your advisor holds the link to Part II and will complete it with you. If you have multiple majors, you must meet with each major advisor and complete a Part II for each. Bring transcripts for any non-Cornell coursework (transfer credit, study abroad).
3
Confirm and follow up
After submitting, monitor your student record and contact the A&S Registrar's Office (as-studentservices@cornell.edu / 607-255-5004) with any issues.
Returning after a long absence? If you've been withdrawn or away for more than five years, email as-studentservices@cornell.edu to determine whether a return is possible.

Graduating Early

  • Possible if you can complete all graduation requirements in fewer than 8 semesters
  • You must still satisfy the college residency requirement
  • Earliest you can officially request early graduation: immediately after pre-enrollment for your anticipated final semester
  • Pre-enroll in your final required courses first, then notify A&S Registrar at as-studentservices@cornell.edu with subject line: Early Graduation
  • After your graduation date is updated, complete Part I and have your advisor complete Part II
Email Registrar — Early Graduation

Graduating Late (9th+ Term)

The BA is expected to be completed in 8 terms. Additional terms are only approved for emergent circumstances beyond the student's control — not for adding a major or minor.

Process for Requesting a 9th Term
1
Faculty advisor updates Part II
Must detail remaining major requirements
2
Submit a study plan to your college advisor
List the specific courses that will meet degree requirements for one major
3
Email the A&S Registrar's Office
Official request for 9th term — as-studentservices@cornell.edu

Honors

Policy change effective December 2026: Cornell will institute a standardized Latin Honors system based solely on final cumulative undergraduate GPA. Existing college-specific honors systems will be discontinued at end of Summer 2026. A&S majors will transition to "Honors in [X]" designations (e.g., Honors in Chemistry).
Bachelor of Arts with Honors (in a Major)
Almost all departments offer honors programs for exceptional major work and research. Requirements — including cum laude, magna cum laude, summa cum laude thresholds — are set by each department. Minors do not offer honors. Contact the DUS for your program.
Bachelor of Arts with Distinction in All Subjects
Requires: 60+ credits at Cornell; GPA in top 30% of class after 7th semester; no grade below C–; no failing grades; no frozen Incompletes; good academic standing for last 4 semesters.
