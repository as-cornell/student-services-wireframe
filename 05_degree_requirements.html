Language Requirement

Two Ways to Fulfill It
What's RequiredClass Roster Code
Option 1Successfully complete one intermediate course (3+ credits) at the 2000 level or above at CornellFLOPI-AS
Option 2Successfully complete at least 11 credits in a single language in the appropriate sequence at Cornell (2–3 semesters)N/A
Key Rules
  • Continuing a language from high school? You must take a placement test — enrollment in the wrong level is not permitted
  • Starting a new language? First courses are typically only offered in the fall semester
  • AP/IB scores provide placement but do not fulfill the language requirement
  • Planning to study abroad in a non-English speaking country? Additional language study beyond intermediate may be required
  • Fluent in another language? Exemptions possible if you completed secondary school in that language, or can demonstrate near-native proficiency by exam
I want to enroll in a lower level than I placed into. Is that okay?
No. Departments require students to enroll in the course indicated by their placement exam results.
Can I study more languages after fulfilling the requirement?
Yes — many students study multiple languages or continue beyond the requirement.
