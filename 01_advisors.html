Academic Advising

Advisors

Every A&S student has access to several kinds of advisors, each supporting a different part of your academic path. Start with your advising dean if you're not sure who to ask.

Types of Advisors

The College of Arts & Sciences supports students through four kinds of advisors, each with a distinct role.

Advising Deans
College of Arts & Sciences
Help you explore academic resources, understand college and university requirements, and think through the curriculum. Your first stop for general academic planning, support resources, or working through a challenge.
Faculty Advisors
Assigned at major declaration
Your primary contact for major requirements and course planning within your major. Find your assigned faculty advisor in Student Center under "program advisor."
Directors of Undergraduate Studies (DUS)
By department
Each department's DUS helps students plan a path toward a specific major, including course sequencing and departmental requirements.
Office of Undergraduate Biology (OUB)
Biology majors — A&S and CALS
Advises biology majors across both the College of Arts & Sciences and CALS on academics and research opportunities.
