AP, IB & A-Level Credits

How exam credit is awarded in the College of Arts & Sciences — what counts, what doesn't, and how to check your record.

Overview

  • Many students enter with AP, IB, A-Level, or French Baccalaureate exam credit
  • Credits are added automatically to your record after Cornell receives official scores — no action needed
  • You may enroll in courses assuming placement even before credits appear on your record
  • If you take an intro course your credit places you out of, you forfeit that AP/IB credit
  • Some departments recommend taking the intro course anyway for subject grounding — check your department's website
For a comprehensive list of all subjects and credits awarded by Cornell, see the University Catalog → Advanced Placement & Standing. The table below covers English exam credit specific to A&S.

Key Rules to Remember

  • AP/IB credit counts as Non-A&S — does not apply toward the 100 required College of Arts & Sciences credits
  • AP/IB scores can be used for placement only toward the language requirement — they do not fulfill it
  • Taking the course the credit places you out of = credit forfeited
  • For pre-health students: AP credit acceptance varies by professional school — confirm with each school you're considering
  • Some departments advise taking the intro course regardless of credit for stronger subject grounding
