Study Abroad

A&S students study abroad to immerse in other cultures, develop language proficiency, and pursue unique academic opportunities. Most study abroad in their junior year or fall of senior year.

Info Session — Planning for Study Abroad in Spring 2027 Wednesday, March 11, 5:00–6:00 p.m. · KG70 Klarman Hall (Rhodes-Rawlings Auditorium) · Session will not be recorded. If unable to attend, schedule with your A&S study abroad advisor.

Overview

With college permission, most A&S students may study abroad for one or two fall or spring semesters on programs approved by the College of Arts & Sciences. Short-term programs (winter, spring break, summer) may be pursued independently and are not subject to A&S policies.

All of the following must be met at the time of application and at departure:

Cannot study abroad: first-year students, students in their final semester, or students who do not meet all eligibility requirements above.

Language Requirement (Spring 2026 and After)

Prior to Departure

If the language of your host country is offered at Cornell:

  • Complete whatever language preparation your program requires — highly regarded programs may require 4–5 semesters of preparation
  • OR if no program language requirement, take a minimum of one Cornell course (for a letter grade) at whatever level you place into — you may not test out of this
You must complete both the A&S general language requirement and any language requirement for your specific program. Many students use the same language for both.
While Abroad

All students in a non-English speaking country must take at least one course in the host country language while abroad.

Important: Study abroad courses cannot fulfill the A&S language requirement — that must be done through Cornell courses.

Planning Timeline

Year 1
Attend information sessions. Complete FWS, work toward language requirement. Begin exploring programs and research host country language requirements — start language coursework if needed.
Year 2
Complete language requirement, declare a major. Research programs in detail. Meet with Education Abroad and A&S study abroad advisors.
Application Deadlines
Spring programs: apply May–September of prior year. Fall/year-long programs: apply February–March of prior year. Note: Oxford and Cambridge deadlines are very early.

Course Approval Process

Study abroad courses may count as elective credit, fulfill distribution requirements, or fulfill major requirements (with major department approval). There are three approval steps.

1
A&S Study Abroad Participation & Course Approval Form
Submitted as part of your Cornell study abroad application. Propose a tentative course list, potential requirements they could fulfill, and a degree completion plan within 8 semesters.
2
Final Course Enrollments & Approvals
Submitted after enrolling in all study abroad courses and confirmed they will not change — often happens after you are already abroad.
3
Final Confirmation of Credit Approval
Submitted after returning and after your study abroad transcript is received by A&S.
Save all syllabi, reading lists, and completed work from your courses abroad — these materials are often required to confirm distribution or major credit.

Coursework & Credits

  • Must enroll in a full course load as defined by the host institution — typically earns 15 Cornell credits
  • All courses must be taken in person and for a letter grade — no online, hybrid, or S/U courses
  • No credit for grades below a C (or equivalent)
  • Maximum of one non-A&S course allowed with prior college approval
  • Courses may fulfill: major/minor requirements (dept. approval needed), distribution requirements (college approval needed), or elective credit
  • Study abroad courses cannot fulfill FWS, language, or PE requirements
  • Internships and experiential learning courses generally do not count for academic credit
  • You may not take Cornell courses remotely during your semester abroad
Grades earned abroad are not calculated into your Cornell GPA.

Transcripts

  • After final grades post, ensure an official transcript is sent from your host institution to the Office of Global Learning
  • Upon receipt, A&S sends a final credit approval form — must be completed before credits are processed
  • All courses and grades appear on your Cornell transcript exactly as they appear on your study abroad transcript
  • Students who don't complete the semester in good academic standing will be reviewed by the College

Study Abroad FAQs

When can I study abroad?
Most students go junior year or fall of senior year. Not allowed: first year, final semester. Most programs don't accept sophomores.
Where can I study abroad?
Any program approved by the College of Arts & Sciences, as long as you meet all eligibility requirements for that program.
Can I fulfill the A&S language requirement abroad?
No — the language requirement must be fulfilled through Cornell courses taken at Cornell.
How will study abroad courses count toward my degree?
They may fulfill major/minor requirements (dept. approval), distribution requirements (college approval), or elective credit. Don't assume any specific requirement will be fulfilled other than the 15 Cornell credits for a full semester.
How will study abroad courses appear on my transcript?
Exactly as they appear on your study abroad transcript — courses and grades both appear on your Cornell transcript. Grades are not factored into your Cornell GPA.
I need to take different courses than I proposed. How do I get approval?
Email your A&S study abroad advisor and major department (for major courses) between Step 1 and Step 2 of the course approval process.
Can I leave early for a summer internship?
No. When you commit to a program, you commit to be there from start to finish.
Who handles housing, finances, and visa questions?
Your Education Abroad advisor — not the A&S study abroad team. Find your advisor on your desired program's snapshot page.
