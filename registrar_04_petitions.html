Petitions to A&S Academic Policies

How to request an exception to college academic policy, organized by petition type.

How to Submit

1
Choose the right form
Online petition form for A&S primary courses and general requests. Paper/PDF General Petition Form only for requests involving non-A&S courses.
2
Submit your statement
For the paper form, email your completed portion and statement to as-studentservices@cornell.edu, cc'ing your faculty advisor, advising dean, and instructor (if an enrollment change is requested).
3
Request advisor/instructor sign-off
Ask your faculty advisor (and instructor, if applicable) to reply with their response directly to as-studentservices@cornell.edu so your petition can be reviewed.

Petition Types

TypeWhen You Must PetitionNotes
OverhoursNot in good standing previous semester and want 18+ credits; or requesting 22+ creditsSubmit only during the regular add/drop period — not accepted for pre-enrollment. Determined by total credits including PE and repeats.
Late AddAdding course(s) after the add deadlineFor A&S independent study/research, use the Independent Study Form instead — not this petition.
Withdrawing from a CourseDropping during the withdrawal period (after day 57)Results in a permanent "W" — may affect academic standing.
Change Grade Option After DeadlineSwitching S/U → letter grade after the deadline, to count toward a major/minorOnly approved if the major/minor specifically requires a letter grade for that course.
Foreign Language ExemptionYour language isn't taught at Cornell and can't be tested here, and your competency matches expected incoming-freshman English levelInclude answers to the Foreign Language Exemption Questionnaire in your statement. For Cornell-taught languages, contact the department directly instead.
Study AbroadRequesting exemption from a college approval requirement for study abroadSee the Study Abroad policy (Education section) for the full eligibility list. Questions: as_studyabroad@cornell.edu
Questions about petition types or the submission process: contact the A&S Registrar's Office. Questions about whether a petition is the right move for your situation, or how to plan around a policy exception: talk to your advising dean.
