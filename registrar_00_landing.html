Registrar Services

The A&S Registrar's Office upholds university and college policy on grading, enrollment, and student records, and certifies that students meet the requirements for the B.A. degree. Located in KG17, Klarman Hall.

This section covers policy, forms, and process. For help deciding what to do — choosing courses, planning a leave, weighing a petition — visit Academic Advising, where your advising dean can talk through your specific situation.

Forms

Submit completed forms as a scan or photo to as-studentservices@cornell.edu. If additional signatures are required (faculty advisor, instructor), copy them and request they email their approval to the same address. Hard copies of all PDF forms are available in KG17 Klarman Hall.
