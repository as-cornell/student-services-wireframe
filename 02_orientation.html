Thinking About a Major

A&S offers 40 majors. You have until the end of your sophomore year to declare. Use your first semesters to explore.

First-Year Students

All A&S first-years start as undeclared. This is typical and intentional — it gives you freedom to explore before committing.

  • Browse the Course Guidance for Major Exploration to find recommended intro courses by field
  • Take courses in 2–3 departments that look most interesting
Transfer Students
  • Most majors have prerequisite courses — check the University Catalog and departmental websites
  • If continuing a major from your previous institution, contact the department to determine how prior courses apply — you may need to provide a syllabus or course description

Enrolling in Courses

  • Use the Class Roster Scheduler to build and save possible schedules before officially enrolling
  • Star ("favorite") courses that interest you — then enroll through Student Center
  • Adding to your shopping cart does not reserve your spot — click "Finish Enrolling"
  • Every course component must be enrolled: lecture, discussion section, and/or lab
  • Course levels: 1000–2000 = introductory (typical for first-years); 3000–4000 = upper-level; 5000+ = graduate (not recommended for first-years)
  • If enrolling in a 3000/4000-level course, check prerequisites and consider contacting the instructor to confirm you have the background
For FWS enrollment, consult the Knight Writing Institute for current guidelines and section availability.

Transfer Credit Evaluation (Transfer Students Only)

Your credit evaluation arrived with your admission letter and shows how prior coursework satisfies Cornell requirements. It creates your DUST record.

If you believe a course should fulfill a college requirement but isn't listed, contact Savannah Williams (ss376@cornell.edu).

AP, IB & A-Level Credits

  • Credits are added to your Student Center automatically once exam scores are received — may take several weeks (July–August for incoming first-years)
  • You may enroll in courses assuming placement credit before credits appear on your record
  • If you take an intro course that your AP/IB credit places you out of, you forfeit that credit
  • Some departments recommend taking the intro course anyway for grounding in methodology — check department websites
  • AP/IB does not fulfill the A&S language requirement (placement only)
