Academic Advising

Placement Exams

Placement exams determine the right starting level in a subject. Grades on these exams do not appear on your university record.

Overview

Based on your exam performance, you may be placed into: the introductory course; a more advanced course (skipping intro, no credit); or a more advanced course and awarded credit for the intro course.

Not sure whether you need to take a placement exam? Consult your advising dean. Students may also enroll in the course they deem most appropriate based on course descriptions before taking a placement test, and adjust enrollment after orientation.

Foreign Languages

Required if you have more than one year of prior study in a language and have no SAT II or AP score in it. Online placement exams are available for: Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Spanish.

Language Exemption

If your proficiency in a non-English language is native or near-native (completed high school in that language, or demonstrated by special exam), you may be exempt from the language requirement. Contact your advising dean.

Language Resource Center (LRC)

The LRC is Cornell's hub for language innovation. Some courses are offered through the Shared Course Initiative with Columbia and Yale. Visit lrc.cornell.edu for details.

Advanced Standing Exams (CASE) are offered for specific intro courses in these disciplines. Consult each department's site for current dates, times, and locations.

Chemistry & Chemical Biology
CASE for CHEM 2070/2090 and CHEM 2080 — offered at start of each Fall and Spring semester. See chemistry.cornell.edu/case-exams
Computer Science
CASE for CS 1110 — offered at start of Fall semester only. See cs.cornell.edu/courses/placement
Mathematics
CASE for MATH 1110, 1120, and 1910 — offered at start of Fall and Spring semesters. See math.cornell.edu/ap
Physics
CASE for PHYS 1112 and PHYS 2213 — offered prior to start of Fall or Spring semester. See physics.cornell.edu/advanced-standing
