Academic Policies

The official rules governing academic integrity, standing, course changes, and leaves of absence in the College of Arts & Sciences.

Need help applying these rules to your situation? Your advising dean can talk through your options before you act.

Academic Integrity

All students are responsible for understanding their rights and responsibilities under Cornell's Code of Academic Integrity. Plagiarism — using or adapting another's words or ideas without acknowledgment — is the most common violation.

Academic Standing

To maintain good academic standing each semester, a student must:

#Requirement
1Complete at least 12 academic credits by semester's end
2Attain a semester GPA of 2.0 or better
3Be admitted into a major by the end of the summer following sophomore year
4Complete two First-Year Writing Seminars within the first four semesters
To stay on pace for 8-semester graduation, students should reach 60 credits by end of sophomore year and 90 credits by end of junior year. Courses on the Courses That Won't Count list do not count toward standing.

Students who don't meet one or more criteria are reviewed by the Academic Records Committee at semester's end. Possible outcomes: warning, elevated warning, or a required leave of absence (one semester or one year).

Returning from Leave

  • Complete the Return from Leave form, with documentation that all return conditions are satisfied (including any Bursar holds)
  • Health leave returns require contacting Cornell Health and working with the Health Leaves Coordinator
  • Submit your return request by March 1 (for fall) or October 1 (for spring) to facilitate pre-enrollment
  • Credit earned while on leave is governed by the Transferring Credits policy
Away more than 5 years or previously withdrawn? Email as-studentservices@cornell.edu — staff will help determine whether a return is possible.
