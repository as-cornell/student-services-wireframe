Registrar Services — Policy

Leave of Absence & Withdrawal

The policy governing how and when a leave or withdrawal takes effect, and what it means for your tuition, transcript, and return eligibility.

Considering a leave? Your advising dean can help you weigh your options and plan your return.
Health Leave? You'll also need to visit Cornell Health Leaves to finalize the request.
Cornell Health Leaves

Effective Dates

  • Submitting the online form date/time-stamps your request. A confirmation email goes to your Cornell address immediately.
  • During an active term: the submission date becomes your official last day of attendance for tuition and grading purposes
  • For a future term not yet started: the effective date is the day after your last date of attendance (typically the day after the term's last final exam)
Leave of Absence & Withdrawal Form

Timing Rules

When RequestedEffect on Transcript & Return Eligibility
Before semester start or by the university drop deadlineUpcoming/current semester's courses are expunged (except completed partial-term classes). Eligible to return next semester, subject to any conditions.
After the drop deadline, through the last day of the termEnrolled classes show "W"s on the transcript. Partial-term courses already completed keep their grade. Can only return after a full semester away, subject to conditions.
If a student on elevated warning requests a leave after the drop deadline, the Academic Records Committee reviews the case and may assign warning, elevated warning, required leave, or withdrawal in place of the personal leave request.

Leaves are granted for a minimum of one or two semesters and expire after five years. Students on leave may not enroll in Cornell courses; courses taken elsewhere in the U.S. may be eligible for transfer credit via petition. Study abroad during a leave does not earn academic credit.

Financial & Enrollment Impact

  • Check with the Bursar's Office, Financial Aid, Housing & Dining, and Student Employment before taking a leave
  • Students with educational loans should contact Financial Aid specifically
  • Medical or auto insurance eligibility may be affected during a leave
  • Outstanding tuition or university charges through the leave date remain the student's responsibility
  • On-campus housing/dining charges may accrue until services are no longer used, regardless of official leave date
